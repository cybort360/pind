# Pind demo script

Target length: 60–75 seconds.

## 0–7 seconds — the tension

Show a chaotic folder/email composition or begin directly on camera:

> “The client says they never approved the final version. Which file did they actually sign off on?”

Cut immediately into Pind.

## 7–18 seconds — establish the workspace

Open `/app`.

- Point to the four real projects and populated activity.
- Open **Summer Packaging Redesign**.
- Say: “Pind keeps every revision, comment, and decision attached to one project.”

## 18–34 seconds — the magical interaction

Open the latest revision.

- Click directly on the artwork.
- Add: “Confirm the origin text is legible at print size.”
- Show the numbered pin appearing on the exact location.
- Resolve an earlier comment and show the studio response.

## 34–51 seconds — client view

Choose **Client view**.

- Switch from revision two to revision three.
- Show the existing resolved feedback.
- Click **Approve revision**.
- Enter the approval note: “Approved for production.”

## 51–62 seconds — payoff

Open the generated receipt.

- Highlight exact revision, filename, client identity, timestamp, and receipt code.
- Say: “Now the approval is a record, not a memory buried in chat.”

## 62–75 seconds — template proof

Open `/app/settings` and change the accent colour or studio name, then open `/design-system`.

Close with:

> “Remix Pind on Replit, replace the branding, connect PostgreSQL, Resend, Cloudinary, or Slack, and launch a working client portal.”

## Recording notes

- Use the seeded demo; do not start from an empty dashboard.
- Keep browser zoom around 90% so the review canvas and feedback panel fit together.
- Record both the studio and client URL.
- Avoid narrating the stack until the final template-proof section.
- The emotional payoff is the approval receipt, not the dashboard metrics.
